## Notes (paste into "Notes")
Letterfold is a single-player puzzle book; no login, no network, no purchases.
To play: drag the page with one finger to close, stand up or tilt the book (two pages touch when it closes); tap a square to walk the figure; reach the flag to deliver the letter. Two-finger swipe left undoes.
Chapters open in order; Settings → Button Control shows on-screen fold/undo buttons, and the whole game is playable with VoiceOver.

(Optional 4th line if review asks for later content: there is no unlock shortcut in release builds — the first chapter alone shows every core interaction.)
